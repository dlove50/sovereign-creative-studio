## How each scene prompt is built (from the original)

1. **Look and format:** "Cinematic realistic jungle adventure, 16:9."
2. **Identity lock:** use the attached portrait as the ONLY identity reference; preserve faces, skin tones, age, hair.
3. **Wardrobe lock, per character** (identical in every scene).
4. **Global rules:** natural action and anatomy, coherent camera cuts, no text or narration, the sound bed.
5. **Scene label and length:** "Opening scene, 15 seconds:".
6. **Beats in order, with camera cues:** what happens, then "Cut to ...", "Front medium close-up of ...", "End with ...".
7. **Continuity guards:** what must or must not happen ("No explosion", "the plane remains damaged and cannot fly").
8. **Closing reminder:** "Keep the faces exactly as the reference; do not substitute other actors."

Generator settings used: 5 s clips, 768p, 16:9, with Multi-Scene Cut, Dynamic Camera Move and Multi-Character Scene.

## Why it works (what Sovereign's writer must copy)

- One-line idea in, a **titled chapter list** out (8 to 10 chapters), each 1 to 3 shots of 3 to 8 seconds.
- A **bible** (look, cast identity, wardrobe, rules, sound) that is pasted into every scene so the people never change.
- **Beats with camera cues** inside each scene, and **continuity guards** that keep the story consistent.
- Rising stakes, and an ending that mirrors the opening.
